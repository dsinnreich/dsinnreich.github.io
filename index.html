About

I'm a Product Manager at Amazon, working on Responsible AI. Today that means building customizable safety tools for the Amazon Nova models: content moderation and safeguards that teams can tune to their own risk posture rather than accept off the shelf controls.

Before AGI I spent several years at AWS on low-code and no-code machine learning, leading product for Amazon SageMaker Canvas and Amazon Forecast. My roles encompassed both GTM and technical product management (PMT) for ML models & service, data pipelines, and generative AI to solve business problems without having to write code.

I came to AI from quant finance. At MSCI I built investment analytics platforms and time-series risk models used by institutional investors managing trillions in assets.

Outside of work, I play hockey, read science fiction, volunteer for various service organizations, and I like traveling to island: Fiji, French Polynesia, Corsica, Sardinia are some of my favorites. A little scuba diving also.
